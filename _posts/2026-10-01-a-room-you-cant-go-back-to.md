Write about a room you can no longer enter: a grandparent's kitchen, a classroom, a hospital corridor, an apartment someone else lives in now. Begin with one object that was there. Stay in the room for the whole piece, and don't leave it to explain.

*Give it twenty minutes and keep it under 500 words.*

## A note on beginning

Most of the writing I love began smaller than it ended. A single object, a sentence overheard, a room. We tend to wait for the whole shape of a piece before we start, and the shape almost never arrives that way. It comes from the doing.

So begin with the object. Describe it plainly, the way you'd describe it to someone who has never been inside that room. Then let it lead you. If you get stuck, go back to the object and look again. There is always more there than you saw the first time.
