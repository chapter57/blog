Most of the writing I love began smaller than it ended. A single object, a
sentence overheard, a room. We tend to wait for the whole shape of a piece
before we start, and the shape almost never arrives that way. It comes from
the doing.

So this month, begin with the object. Describe it plainly, the way you'd
describe it to someone who has never been inside that room. Then let it lead
you. If you get stuck, go back to the object and look again. There is always
more there than you saw the first time.

Write to us and tell us how it went.
